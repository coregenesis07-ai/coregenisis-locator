# Federal Custody Guide / Locator — Findings

## Operating principles
- Keep searches and educational information easy to understand.
- Distinguish official/public-source data from estimates or educational calculations.
- Avoid implying legal advice or guaranteed release outcomes.
- Prioritize mobile readability and bilingual consistency.

## UI/UX review criteria
- Strong language toggle behavior
- Clear search labels and result states
- Calculator inputs grouped logically
- Results explain assumptions
- Navigation always provides a clear route home
- Accessible contrast, spacing, and touch targets

## Reliability notes
- Blank screens are release blockers.
- Silent zero-result behavior is not acceptable; explain what happened.
- Calculator changes require regression testing against known cases.
