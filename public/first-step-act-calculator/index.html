First Step Act Planning Calculator

Model selected FSA Time Credits, approximate maximum Good Conduct Time (GCT), and an optional RDAP reduction using information you enter.

Planning estimate only: this calculator does not determine FSA eligibility, award credits, modify a sentence, or replace the Bureau of Prisons' official sentence computation. The BOP itself describes its conditional FSA calculator as a planning tool rather than an actual Projected Release Date.

Enter the sentence information

Use 15 days only when the BOP record supports the additional earning rate. BOP guidance says eligible people generally earn 10 days per 30 days, with an additional 5 days for qualifying minimum/low risk status over the required assessments.

Entering an RDAP reduction does not determine RDAP eligibility. Use only an amount you want to model for planning.

What the result means

Sentence end before credits: calendar estimate based on the start date and imposed sentence length you enter.

Approximate GCT: a planning estimate using the statutory maximum rate. Actual BOP calculations and disciplinary history control.

Modeled FTC: the number of 30-day periods you enter multiplied by 10 or 15 days.

Illustrative combined date: a planning comparison only. It is not an official release date, prerelease placement date, or supervised-release date.

Companion worksheet

BOP Security / Custody Score Worksheet

This worksheet totals point values that you already have from a BOP form or have verified under the current classification policy. It intentionally does not assign policy points automatically because current BOP classification can involve detailed tables, exceptions, overrides and professional judgment.

BOP states that designation/classification decisions consider security and program needs and are made under its classification policy. Compare your worksheet with the current PS 5100.08 and official BOP forms.
