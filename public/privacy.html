Privacy Notice / Aviso de Privacidad

Effective: October 3, 2026

Current status: Alert enrollment and monitoring are not currently available. This site is not accepting alert subscription information.

Information used by the site

The inmate-search feature sends the search term needed to retrieve publicly available Federal Bureau of Prisons locator information. Alert enrollment is disabled, and the current alert pages do not accept subscriber email addresses, telephone numbers, or alert enrollment information.

Why information is used

Search information is used to return public custody-locator results. The current site does not provide alert monitoring or notification enrollment.

Service providers and sources

The application uses Cloudflare infrastructure to serve the site and its search service. It retrieves public locator information from BOP.gov. Alert enrollment and email delivery are not currently active.

No sale statement

The reviewed application code does not contain advertising pixels or code designed to sell subscriber information. This statement describes the reviewed code and does not replace a review of production vendor settings.

Retention and control

The current site does not accept new alert enrollment information. If alerts are introduced in the future, this notice will be updated before enrollment opens to describe retention, deletion, consent, and unsubscribe controls.

Children

Federal Custody Guide is a general-audience educational service and is not directed to children under 13. Alert enrollment is not currently available. Do not attempt to submit personal information for a child under 13.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the service, but no Internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Government independence

Federal Custody Guide is an independent educational resource. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Federal Bureau of Prisons or another government agency.

Contact and privacy requests

A dedicated privacy contact channel is not yet published. Because alert enrollment is disabled, this page does not invite submission of alert subscription information.

Resumen en español
